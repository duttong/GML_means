PART 1 · THE PROBLEM

Meet CFC-11

CCl3F
trichlorofluoromethane
1 carbon · 1 fluorine ·
3 chlorine atoms

CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons) were invented in 1928 as “miracle” chemicals: non-toxic, non-flammable and cheap. By the 1970s they were everywhere.

Fridges
A/C
Spray cans
Foam

The catch: CFCs are so stable that nothing in the lower air breaks them down. CFC-11 lasts about 52 years — plenty of time to drift up to the ozone layer.

How chlorine wrecks ozone

  1. 1
    Drift up. Over years, CFCs rise into the stratosphere, home of the ozone layer.
  2. 2
    Break. UV light frees a chlorine atom.
    CCl3F + UV → Cl + CCl2F
  3. 3
    Attack. Chlorine tears ozone apart.
    Cl + O3 → ClO + O2
  4. 4
    Repeat. The chlorine is set free to strike again.
    ClO + O → Cl + O2
100,000
ozone molecules can be destroyed by one chlorine atom before it is finally locked away.

Earth’s sunscreen

Ozone (O3) absorbs most of the Sun’s UV-B rays, which cause sunburn, skin cancer and cataracts, and harm crops and sea life.

The ozone hole. In 1985, scientists found ozone over Antarctica plunging each spring, as frigid clouds there supercharge chlorine’s attack. The “hole” still returns every September to November.

PART 3 · SCIENCE IN ACTION

Case file: the CFC-11 mystery

After 2012, CFC-11’s fall suddenly slowed by half. Clue #2: northern air pulled further ahead of southern air. Someone in the north was making CFC-11 again, despite the ban. NOAA sounded the alarm in 2018; studies traced much of it to eastern China, and emissions soon dropped.

Extra CFC-11 in northern vs. southern air (ppt)

How NOAA samples the air

At 14 remote stations from the Arctic to the South Pole, clean air is pumped into flasks and shipped to NOAA’s lab in Boulder, Colorado.

Each band is averaged, then the four bands are averaged into one global number.

Report card

Drop since each gas peaked, to 2025. Short-lived gases vanish fastest.
